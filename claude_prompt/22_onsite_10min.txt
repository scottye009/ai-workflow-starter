10-MINUTE ONSITE STARTER

REPO / MATERIAL: [PATH]
TASK: [QUESTION]

I have limited time. Do not start coding immediately.

In the first pass:
1. Understand the user/business outcome
2. Identify expected vs actual behavior
3. Inspect only the most relevant files/data
4. State confirmed facts, assumptions, and unknowns
5. Identify likely failure/implementation layer
6. Propose the smallest testable next step

Keep analysis concise.
Ask only questions that would materially change what we do next.
Do not overengineer.
