RAG EVALUATION

Evaluate RETRIEVAL and GENERATION separately.

Retrieval:
Recall@K = relevant evidence retrieved / all relevant evidence
Precision@K = relevant retrieved / K
Hit rate = any relevant result retrieved?
MRR = how early first relevant result appears

Generation:
- groundedness
- correctness
- completeness
- citation correctness
- abstention when evidence is insufficient

Debug:
Correct evidence missing -> retrieval issue
Correct evidence present but answer wrong -> generation/reasoning issue
