CHECKPOINTING / RESUME

Use persistence when:
- tasks are long-running
- API/model calls are expensive
- workflows pause for human review
- retries should resume instead of restart
- you need auditability

Important:
Checkpointing does NOT automatically mean "do not rerun completed work" for every invocation pattern.
Understand how your graph is invoked and whether you are supplying fresh input that restarts execution.

Use:
- stable thread_id
- graph/model/data version when stale checkpoints are risky
- explicit reset/recompute policy
- careful handling of PHI / sensitive state
